About

Recruiting tech talent with the full cycle in mind

I'm an IT Talent Acquisition Specialist with 4+ years of full-cycle recruitment, in-house and agency side, across 10+ countries including Morocco, Canada, France, Switzerland, the UAE and South Africa.

I've hired for global tech and telecom teams at TELUS Digital, placed IT consultants with luxury clients like Rolex and Patek Philippe through Experis in Geneva, recruited finance profiles for French clients at Hays, and staffed engineering projects in energy, aeronautics and oil & gas at Fortil.

I know both sides of the job: the in-house rhythm of intake, interviews and onboarding, and the agency model of client briefs, salary portage and placement at the end client.
